# Plane Terrain

Full-bleed sheet of evenly spaced left→right fibers over scattered hills and valleys.

## Look

- One plane spanning the view (left edge ↔ right edge)
- Parametric L→R lines with even gaps in plane space
- Each fiber follows a heightfield of soft pockets + fbm grain
- Connect coral line palette on a cool light ground
